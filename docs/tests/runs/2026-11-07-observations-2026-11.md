## Findings

1. PDFs printed a void on its UTC day (SAL-11, COT-13, RC-08, PAY-08): `Printed::date()` formatted the moment unconverted. Fixed in `8c8aeae` (`Printed::moment`, PrintedTest).
2. An emitted document lost a tax or payment method deactivated since (TAX-12): the editor loaded only active ones. Fixed in `8c8aeae` (`?all=1`, a select offers active ones plus the row's own).
3. The read-only lines grid overlapped (DOC-09, COD-05): widths by `nth-child` shifted when Código was added. Fixed in `8c8aeae` (`doc-lines--coded`).
4. A product code was re-read from the product on a draft save and on order conversion (COD-03, OC-12). Fixed in `8c8aeae`: a line keeps its code for that product; conversion passes the order's.
5. Wide report PDFs were cut off (IMP-11, EXO-07). Fixed in `8c8aeae`: landscape and smaller type above six columns.
6. The Caja y bancos hint did not wrap (REP-19); the balance de prueba of an empty period said it balanced (LED-08). Fixed in `8c8aeae`.
7. Outside the cases: an unknown `/reportes/impuestos/…` address looped (relative `<Navigate>`). Fixed in `8c8aeae`, with a test.
8. CIU-09 expected stacking on a tablet, against DESIGN.md (≤ 600px): the case was corrected, not the app.
9. Left as follow-ups: tercero search in purchase and sales documents ignores role; the quick-create tercero modal on a sale requires Correo; «Sin auxiliar por defecto» badge does not count products (CTA-09); a 2025 invoice voided in 2026 counts in 1008 (its reversal is dated 2026).
